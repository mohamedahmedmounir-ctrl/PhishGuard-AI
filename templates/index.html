HOW IT WORKS

PhishGuard AI uses a two-layer detection approach:

01
ML Classification — A Naive Bayes model trained on TF-IDF vectors extracted from thousands of phishing and legitimate emails. It learns statistical patterns of phishing language.
02
Heuristic Analysis — Rule-based feature extraction identifies urgency language, credential requests, brand impersonation, suspicious URLs, and aggressive formatting.
03
Blended Scoring — Final risk score = 70% ML confidence + 30% heuristic risk multiplier, giving a robust score even for novel phishing variations.
